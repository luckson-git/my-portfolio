How the analysis works

A typed table preserves the 35 source columns. Primary keys and constraints protect the import. A reusable view defines tenure bands; conditional aggregates, CTEs, DENSE_RANK, NTILE, and a CROSS JOIN support ten analyses. All 51,450 imported values were compared with the CSV, and the queries were executed on MySQL 8.0.44.

What to investigate next

Compare overtime within job roles and tenure bands, review group sizes, and collect a defined reporting period before calculating turnover over time. Use real, authorized longitudinal data before drawing operational conclusions.

Source: IBM HR Analytics dataset listing, described as fictional. This snapshot has no reporting dates; rates are not annual turnover and comparisons do not establish causality.
